<article class="manual"><div class="manual-meta">ABOUT THE INDEX</div><h1>Make the web more useful.</h1><div class="prose"><p>Tools and How Tos is for people who want a straightforward answer before they add another tab, subscription, or workflow to their day. We review online tools and write practical guides for makers, small teams, creators, and operators.</p><p>We cover software, SaaS products, ecommerce workflows, automation, and the small utilities that remove repetitive work. A useful recommendation explains what a tool does well, where it is a poor fit, and how to begin without ceremony.</p><h2>How we work</h2><p>Articles favor real tasks, clear tradeoffs, and steps readers can follow. We update guidance as products change, distinguish opinions from facts, and link to primary sources whenever possible. Editorial judgment is independent: a product mention is not a promise that it fits every workflow.</p><h2>What readers can expect</h2><p>We focus on the moment before a decision: choosing a tool, setting it up, finding the limit, or simplifying a workflow that has become too elaborate. The aim is not to collect software. It is to leave each reader with a better next move.</p></div></article>
